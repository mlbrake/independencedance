About Us

Founded and led by Owner & Artistic Director Heather Brake, Independence Dance was built on a simple premise: dance instruction should inspire confidence and artistic excellence without unnecessary financial friction.

Under Heather's creative direction, our curriculum balances rigorous technical development with personal artistic expression, creating a studio environment where dancers of every level thrive.

  • Zero Advanced Commitments
  • Supportive, Inclusive Atmosphere
  • Focus on Technique & Artistry

Pay As You Go

We do not require advanced payments or multi-month locked contracts. Pay only when you dance, keeping flexibility firmly in your hands.

Our Classes

Ballet

Develop poise, balance, alignment, and core strength through classical techniques adapted for modern dancers.

Contemporary & Jazz

Combine athletic movement, expressive storytelling, dynamic leaps, and floorwork in a fluid environment.

Acro

Merge classic dance technique with precision acrobatics, building flexibility, tumbling skills, and seamless transitions.

Strength & Conditioning

Targeted cross-training to build stamina, core balance, joint stability, and injury prevention for dancers of all levels.
